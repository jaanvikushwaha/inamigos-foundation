About InAmigos Foundation

InAmigos Foundation was founded on 23rd September 2020 by Mr. Govind Shukla, Founder & CEO. It is a Section 8 registered non-profit organization based in Chhattisgarh and working across India through professionals, volunteers, and community initiatives.


The foundation works towards creating meaningful social impact through education, food and clothing support, animal welfare, women empowerment, environmental conservation, and skill development.

Our Key Initiatives

Project Seva

Project Seva provides food and clothing support to underprivileged communities, helping meet essential needs and promoting dignity and social inclusion.

Project Bachpanshala

Project Bachpanshala works to bridge educational gaps by supporting underprivileged children through school education, basic digital literacy, life skills, and learning support.

Project Jeev

Project Jeev focuses on animal welfare through feeding, rescue, protection, medical support, and awareness for stray and abandoned animals.

Project Udaan

Project Udaan supports women empowerment through skill development, self-help groups, financial independence, and menstrual hygiene awareness.

Project Prakriti

Project Prakriti promotes environmental conservation and sustainability through tree plantation, eco-friendly agriculture, and environmental awareness.

Project Vikas

Project Vikas focuses on employability and skill development through internships and training in areas including data operations, finance, research, content writing, digital marketing, social work, and more.

Our Social Impact

InAmigos Foundation works to create practical and lasting social impact through community-driven initiatives across India. Its programs focus on education, basic needs, environmental sustainability, animal welfare, women empowerment, and employability.

  • Providing food and clothing support to underprivileged communities.
  • Supporting education and digital literacy for children.
  • Promoting women's skill development and financial independence.
  • Supporting animal welfare and feeding stray animals.
  • Encouraging tree plantation and sustainable environmental practices.
  • Providing internships and skill-development opportunities for young people.
